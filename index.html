Yahtzee Rules

Goal: Score the most points by rolling 5 dice to make specific combinations over 13 rounds.

Turn Structure:

  • You have up to 3 rolls per turn.
  • Click on dice you want to hold before rolling again.
  • Select an available box on your scorecard to record your score before your turn ends.

Upper Section Bonus:

If the subtotal of Upper Section scores (Ones through Sixes) is 63 or higher, you receive a +35 point bonus.

Yahtzee & Bonus / Joker Rules:

  • A Yahtzee is 5 of a kind (50 pts).
  • Additional Yahtzees give a +100 Bonus each!
  • Joker Rule: If you roll a bonus Yahtzee and its Upper category is filled, you can use it as a Joker for Full House (25), Small Straight (30), Large Straight (40), or any open category.
